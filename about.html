About Yi-Hsien Huang

Decision Intelligence for Sustainable Industrial Systems · Academic–Industry Bridge

Academic profile

Yi-Hsien Huang is a Taiwan-based researcher and educator working at the intersection of decision science, industrial sustainability, statistical analysis, and infrastructure risk. His work emphasizes transparent analytical assumptions, source-traceable evidence, and the careful evaluation of decision robustness.

His academic background spans manufacturing information and systems, business administration, and environmental engineering. His experience also connects classroom teaching with industrial technology assessment and decision support.

Research interests

  • Decision intelligence and multi-criteria decision making (MCDM)
  • Industrial carbon-performance measurement and accountability
  • Electricity infrastructure disruption, risk, and recovery
  • Statistical decision support, design of experiments, and reliability analysis
