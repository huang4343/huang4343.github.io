Profile

Yi-Hsien Huang, Ph.D. works at the intersection of decision intelligence, sustainability measurement, risk analysis, and industrial systems.

The research approach emphasizes transparent assumptions, traceable evidence, and carefully bounded conclusions.

Research interests

  • Industrial carbon-performance assessment
  • Energy-system risk and recovery
  • Statistical decision support and design of experiments
