Responsible research practice

Research Data

Approaches to transparent methods, reproducibility, and responsible data sharing in quantitative industrial research.

Data and reproducibility principles

Traceable evidence

Record data provenance, analytical definitions, reference periods, and material assumptions so that conclusions can be independently assessed.

Reproducible analysis

Document analysis steps, software environments, and sensitivity checks wherever sharing and reuse rights allow.

Responsible sharing

Respect third-party data rights, organizational confidentiality, and publisher or editorial requirements before sharing research material.

Project-specific research materials and code may be made available through appropriate scholarly channels when the applicable publication and sharing requirements permit.
