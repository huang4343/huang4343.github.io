Academic profile

Research

Research directions and questions; ongoing projects are not represented as published findings.

Industrial sustainability

Carbon-performance accountability

How robust are corporate decarbonization classifications when denominator choices, historical restatements, reporting boundaries, or accounting conventions change?

Methods: longitudinal comparison, sensitivity analysis, decomposition, and claim-level source tracing.

Research stream: semiconductor manufacturing carbon-accountability studies. Publication status is managed separately.

Infrastructure risk

Electric power resilience & recovery

How can outage-event evidence, recovery times, and censored observations inform more careful resilience assessment?

Methods: event cohort construction, survival / accelerated failure-time modeling, and source auditing.

Research stream: U.S. electricity-system disruption and recovery. Ongoing research; not a claim of peer-reviewed acceptance.

Decision science

Robust MCDM & industrial analytics

How should decision-makers test the sensitivity and defensibility of conclusions in uncertain, policy-sensitive or resource-constrained environments?

Methods: MCDM, statistical diagnostics, scenario analysis, and experimental design.

Research statements describe interests and active questions, not validated novelty claims or accepted journal results.
